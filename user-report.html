2. What changes for you right now

Will customer ETAs change?No. ETAs keep using trusted data only. The noisy feed is monitored, not used.

How to accept, correct, or escalate

  1. Look at the evidence panel: the ETA, its expected range, how old the area's history is, and how much comes from the unreliable feed.
  2. Accept if the ETA looks right for that area and time of day.
  3. Correct by typing your own ETA if you know better (traffic, road works, a building that's slow to access). Add a short reason if you can.
  4. Escalate if something looks broken — e.g. many strange ETAs in one area. This goes straight to the data-quality owner.

Your corrections are logged and reviewed by the data team. They are not fed into the model automatically — that would create a new way for bad data to get in.
